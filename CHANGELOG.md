# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-22

First release.

- A walkable hillside in Provence rendered as a living oil painting: world-anchored brush strokes
  laid along each surface's flow, impasto lit by the scene's own light, outlines, and a slow ripple.
- The sky of *The Starry Night*, with its eleven stars and crescent moon, repainted stroke by
  stroke as you watch.
- Four times of day (*The Starry Night*, dawn, day, dusk) on a running clock.
- A dream that comes when you stand still: quickening swirls, heaving hills, drifting colour, and
  lines from Vincent's letters.
- A generative soundscape, fullscreen, and mouse capture inside the painting.
